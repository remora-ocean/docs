amrex::Geometry nonperiodic_geometry ( const amrex::Geometry & geom)
inline

A copy of geom with no periodic direction, for interpolating coordinates from a coarser level. Coordinates do not wrap, so a periodic copy of the coarse data is wrong for them: a coarse ghost cell that also lies beyond a wall would get a second source, its periodic image, with the winner undefined. Note that a coarse level's coordinate ghost cells across a periodic edge are still filled by a periodic FillBoundary, so they hold values a domain width away; an interpolater must not read them there.
